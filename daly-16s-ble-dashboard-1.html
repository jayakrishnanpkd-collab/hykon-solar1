          Protection settings review

          Values are as shown in the Daly app on 15 Nov 2026 (read-only, nothing here changes the BMS). Recommended values come from Daly's published LiFePO4 specification (cell over-voltage 3.65 V ±0.05 V, "do not set higher than the cell specification", charge below 45 C) and typical LiFePO4 cell datasheets. The cell maker's datasheet is the final authority, so ask Hykon for it and compare.
